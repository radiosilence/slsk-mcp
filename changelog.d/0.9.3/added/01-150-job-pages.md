- **A page for each job.** `/jobs/<id>` holds what is known of one job: its card, the source it is fetching from, the other copies found, every outcome it has reached and why, and the import log. Its history is kept after the job is removed, so the page still answers what happened. Cards link to it, and a card waiting on a decision opens its sources and history in place, so a failed or suspect job shows which copy went wrong and what is left to try.
