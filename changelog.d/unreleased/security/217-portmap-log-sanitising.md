- **The UPnP port mapper logs less and retries sooner.** A failed mapping logs one line of `upnpc`'s error output, reduced to printable ASCII and capped, rather than all of its output, which included the WAN address and text any LAN device could forge. With no route out, it retries in a minute instead of running `upnpc` with no address and waiting half a lease. The root init container now also runs with a read-only root filesystem.
