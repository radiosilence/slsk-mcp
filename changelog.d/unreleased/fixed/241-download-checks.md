- **Download checks are answered when the peer refused first.** A peer running Nicotine+'s ProveIt plugin refuses every file with "Verification required" and sends its check, often in that order, so by the time the check arrived nothing was counted as waiting and it went unanswered. Refused downloads now count, the refused files are asked for again once the check is answered, and checks worded with `say`, `reply with`, `the word`, `> … <` marks or several words are recognised.
