- **Job cards show what is being fetched and the evidence for a choice.** Every card is headed by the peer's folder rather than the search that found it. A download expands to each file's progress. A card waiting on a choice lists the downloaded files with their tags and a MusicBrainz search for the album they name, and compares each candidate release track by track (titles that differ, lengths off by seconds, tracks with no file, files with no track), so the choice can be made from the card.
